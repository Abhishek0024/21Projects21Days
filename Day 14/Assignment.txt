Question: What does an SFTTrainer does? What does packing = false means? how do they help in training?

SFTTrainer:
A specialized trainer for supervised fine-tuning of large language models. It handles:
Tokenization
Batching
Training loop and evaluation
Checkpointing
PEFT (parameter-efficient fine-tuning) adapters
This makes it easier to fine-tune LLMs on custom datasets.


packing=False:
Keeps each training example as a separate sequence with its own start/end tokens.

If True, multiple examples are concatenated into one sequence to reduce padding and improve GPU efficiency.


How they help:
SFTTrainer simplifies the fine-tuning process, while packing=False preserves the integrity of individual examples—important for instruction-following or dialogue tasks—though it may use more memory than packed sequences.
